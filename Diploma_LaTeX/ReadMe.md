### General information
The formatting MUST be checked after the paper is finished. 
While most elements were formatted properly at the very beginning there is a possibility some (especially indentation rules) were missed.

## Sources
When writing the paper the sources should be immediately updated! 
at the very least with a __link__ to the source, __access date__ (date when it was read)
and __reference__ in the text.
This way we're minimizing the chances of accidental plagiarism.
